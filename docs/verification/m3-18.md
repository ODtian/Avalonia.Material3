# M3-18 verification — image browsing and refresh

## Agreed seam and evidence policy

Seams are the spec's already confirmed gallery/equivalent host, real input, public component/binding/item state, visible images/geometry and automation providers, plus fresh isolated versioned package consumption. No private methods, private template names, internal collaborators or business services are mocked. `TaskCompletionSource` controls the **host** asynchronous operation boundary, never a library collaborator.

Pinned reference and implementation differences are enumerated in [component contract](../components/m3-18.md). Do not treat functional four-form coverage as exact upstream Arrangement/KeylineList/Morph/fling/spring parity.

## Not verified or deliberately not implemented

- **Not verified:** human Narrator/NVDA/TalkBack reading order/announcements, physical touch or stylus hardware, Android/macOS/Linux native backends, actual platform IME/font fallback quality, AOT/trimming, public NuGet publication or final stable API compatibility. Native UIA does not prove human screen-reader experience.
- **Implementation boundaries:** no AndroidX exhaustive cost/keyline solver, Android/Compose velocity decay, spring/overshoot equality, exact refresh busy-spinner stroke/playback, Compose nested-scroll handoff or upstream Morph engine. See the explicit per-form contract table; these are not presented as mere missing evidence.
- No carousel virtualization, autonomous slideshow, image decoder/cache, business result service or refresh timeout. This does not extend the library into App image/detail gestures or download/search/session behavior.
- #20 owns aggregate gallery registration, final release/API/AOT/platform/manual evidence and mother-spec sign-off. The real App package upgrade remains its external dependency, not silently substituted by BrowseHost.
