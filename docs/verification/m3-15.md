## Limits / remaining platform acceptance

Human NVDA/Narrator reading/order, physical touch hardware, Android/macOS/Linux, AOT/trimming, release/API baseline and final aggregate gallery remain unverified / owned by #20 and the respective hosts. Do not close those criteria from these tests. The App repository has no runnable client; this ticket does not claim M11 product package upgrade.

Normative evidence is pinned token/recipe/interaction projection, not formal equivalence to all upstream pixels/frames/spring travel/native shadow behavior. Fixed headers must have space for48-DIP targets; hosts select scrollable/adaptive layouts and own fonts/assets/localization. Rail here is non-modal; drawer/modal routing/scrims are not inserted into this ticket. The library contains no product navigation/business logic.
